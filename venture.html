Technical Venture — Piso WiFi Vendo Business

Designs, deploys, and maintains a piso wifi vendo hotspot business, running since early 2026.

Infrastructure

  • Starlink (primary connection)
  • TP-Link smart switch with VLAN segmentation
  • Two COMFAST access points (CF-EW87, CF-EW85)

Highlights

  • Traced recurring customer connectivity drops to AP channel interference combined with a WiFi 6 client compatibility bug, and resolved it by pinning channels and downgrading network mode
  • Planning an infrastructure upgrade path — replacing an Orange Pi One with a mini PC, and expanding switch capacity with a TP-Link TL-SG105E
