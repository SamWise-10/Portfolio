Wireless ISP Operations

Technical Venture — Piso WiFi Vendo Business

Designs, deploys, and maintains a piso wifi vendo hotspot business in Bongao, Tawi-Tawi, running since early 2026.

  • Starlink Primary Backhaul
  • TP-Link Smart Switch (VLAN)
  • COMFAST CF-EW87
  • COMFAST CF-EW85

Network Infrastructure

Satellite Backhaul

Starlink primary low-earth-orbit connection delivering low-latency broadband in Bongao, Tawi-Tawi.

Switching & Segmentation

TP-Link smart switch configured with VLAN segmentation to isolate management and client traffic.

Outdoor Wireless Access

Dual high-power COMFAST outdoor access points (CF-EW87 and CF-EW85) tuned for dense client concurrency.

Highlights & Engineering Roadmap

  • Traced recurring customer connectivity drops to AP channel interference combined with a WiFi 6 client compatibility bug, and resolved it by pinning channels and downgrading network mode
  • Planning an infrastructure upgrade path — replacing an Orange Pi One with a mini PC, and expanding switch capacity with a TP-Link TL-SG105E
